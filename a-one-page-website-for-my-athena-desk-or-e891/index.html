Athena Desk Orb

A glowing ESP32 orb that sits on your desk and acts as a little ambient companion for your PC. It notices when you're there, adapts to the room's light, and talks to your computer over MQTT.

Features

LED ring glow

Colour and brightness show status and mood, driven by messages from the PC.

OLED readout

Small display for time, status text and notifications.

Presence sensing

A radar sensor detects when you sit down or walk away, even when you're sitting still.

Ambient light aware

The light sensor dims the orb at night and brightens it by day.

MQTT link

Publishes sensor data and subscribes to commands, so the PC and orb stay in sync over Wi-Fi.
