01 · What it does

A desk companion that pays attention.

Seven jobs, one small orb.

Presence-aware

An mmWave radar notices you sitting down — even when you're perfectly still — and wakes the orb. Walk away and it settles back to a quiet idle.

HLK-LD2410C

Ambient light aware

Reads the room in lux so the glow dims at night and lifts in daylight.

BH1750

Room climate

Temperature, humidity and pressure, measured in an external pod away from the warm electronics.

BME280

Listens

A digital I²S MEMS microphone gives Athena ears on your desk.

INMP441

Shows status

A 0.96″ OLED on the front of the base spells out what the orb is doing.

SSD1306

Glows with Athena

Sixteen LEDs chase and pulse for listening, thinking and speaking.

WS2812B ×16

Controls your desk light

Switches and dims a 12 V warm-white COB strip on command.

IRLZ44N

02 · How it works

Four hops from sensor to assistant.

The orb is a thin Wi-Fi client. Everything it senses and every command it receives travels through a Mosquitto broker running on the PC.

04 · Power

One 12 V input, two rails.

A fused 12 V feed is split: a buck converter makes 5 V for the logic and LED ring, while the desk strip runs straight from 12 V through a logic-level MOSFET.
